      FAQ

      Questions we often hear

      What does 404FoundersLab do?

      We are a product studio. We build, deploy and operate our own software platforms, currently Assetly and SchoolStack.

      Can each client's data be kept separate?

      Yes. Both platforms are multi-tenant. Assetly isolates tenants with row-level security and can use a dedicated database per client. SchoolStack separates enterprises and schools.

      Who is Assetly for?

      IT companies that need one place for devices, the people who hold them and the costs behind them.

      Who is SchoolStack for?

      Single schools and school groups that want admissions, academics, fees, transport and notices in one system.
